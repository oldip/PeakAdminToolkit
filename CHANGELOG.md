# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First stable release.
- Remove experimental remote full recovery from player cards and its native status requests.
- Keep own full recovery and normal/no-penalty revival for players.
- Client time adjustment and whole-team endpoint travel verified in multiplayer.

## 0.8.4

- Full recovery is available while alive, injured, cursed, unconscious or dead.
- Living recovery avoids revival; unconscious recovery wakes first. Held items and backpacks remain untouched in these paths.
- Remote recovery remains an experimental native request.


## 0.8.3

- Remove the Host-transfer attempt from Overview.

- Client time synchronization and team travel requests.
- Time presets: 05:00, 13:00, 21:00 and 01:00.
- Horn HUD correction recovers after temporary item errors.
- Own complete revival clears curse/curable statuses and refills base stamina.
- Experimental remote complete-revival control using native revival/status requests.


## 0.8.2

- Host team travel to the active Void exit portal.
- Endpoint-specific button and result messages in all three interface languages.


## 0.8.1 — Initial public release

- Item cards, categories, multilingual search and generation.
- Local God Mode, infinite stamina, flight, fall protection and status cleanup.
- Player revival, teleport and Host dropped-item recovery.
- Host time presets and team travel.
- English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese interface.
- MIT license, build scripts and translation contribution support.
